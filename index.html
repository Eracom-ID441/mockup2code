ABOUT US.

OUR ATELIER OFFERS TAILORING AND REPAIR OF ALL TYPES OF CLOTHING, BOTH FROM LIGHT FABRICS AND KNITWEAR, AS WELL AS FROM LEATHER AND FUR. WE ARE READY TO SEW TO ORDER ANY THING FOR AN ADULT AND A CHILD, EXACTLY FOLLOWING THE MODEL YOU HAVE CHOSEN. WE GUARANTEE A PERFECT FIT, EXCELLENT QUALITY AND WEARABILITY OF PRODUCTS, BECAUSE IN OUR ATELIER FOR TAILORING AND REPAIRING CLOTHES, ALL ORDERS ARE FIRST WORKED OUT BY TECHNOLOGISTS, AND ONLY THEN TRANSFERRED TO THE SEWING WORKSHOP.

we are a team of inspired fashion creators.
